# BIMO AI original icon and sound assets

BIMO AI uses the original Qanot wing icon and procedural notification sounds.
Historical asset directories and generator filenames remain compatible.
Mochi-based Moshi artwork is governed separately by LICENSE-ASSETS.md.

Copyright (c) 2026 Ahmadjon0worker

This license covers the original Qanot wing/diamond icon design and generated
PNG/ICO artwork, the original Qanot companion artwork, and the 29 procedural WAV
interface cues in `windows/assets/qanot/sounds`. The icon and audio can be
regenerated with `windows/scripts/gen-icons.mjs` and
`windows/scripts/generate-qanot-sounds.mjs`.

The cues are synthesized solely from harmonic oscillators, frequency glides and
amplitude envelopes. They contain no recordings, sampled instruments or copied
upstream audio. Generic event filenames remain for code compatibility.

The reused PNG/ICO encoding helpers and the application's upstream source code
retain their existing MIT attribution in `LICENSE`. Third-party models, engines
and dependencies retain their own licenses; this asset license does not replace
those notices.
