TRAINING DATA
================================================================================

1) Uzbek Speech Corpus (USC) — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
   Required attribution:
     "Our product uses ISSAI and TUIT Uzbek Speech Corpus, which is available
      under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License."
   Citation:
     Musaev, M., Mussakhojayeva, S., Khujayorov, I., Khassanov, Y., Ochilov, M.,
     & Varol, H. A. (2021). USC: An Open-Source Uzbek Speech Corpus and Initial
     Speech Recognition Experiments. arXiv:2107.14419.
   Source:  https://issai.nu.edu.kz/uzbek-asr/
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2) FLEURS (Uzbek), official TRAIN split only — CC BY 4.0
   Citation:
     Conneau, A., Ma, M., Khanuja, S., Zhang, Y., Axelrod, V., Dalmia, S.,
     Riesa, J., Rivera, C., & Bapna, A. (2022). FLEURS: Few-shot Learning
     Evaluation of Universal Representations of Speech.
   Source:  https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Note: only the official TRAIN split is used; the disjoint TEST split is held
         out for evaluation and is NOT part of the training data.

3) Common Voice (Uzbek) — CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
   Mozilla Common Voice.  https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/
   No attribution is required; acknowledged here with thanks.

4) UzbekVoice — Apache License 2.0
   Crowd-sourced Uzbek speech corpus by the UzbekVoice / Mohir AI initiative.
   Source:  https://uzbekvoice.ai/

================================================================================
EXCLUSIONS
================================================================================
Machine pseudo-labels, scraped audio, and the "feruza" and "omni" evaluation
sets are explicitly excluded from the training data. No evaluation benchmark
test set is present in training.
